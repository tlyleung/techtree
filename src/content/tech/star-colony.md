A star colony is a permanent human settlement on a planet or moon orbiting another star. It would be the point at which humanity stopped depending on a single solar system, and it is the natural capstone of a tree of technology.

Every part of it is far beyond current technology. The nearest star is more than four light years away; Voyager 1, the fastest spacecraft leaving the Solar System, would take tens of thousands of years to get there. Settlers would need a ship that keeps people alive for generations, a closed ecosystem far more reliable than any yet tested (Biosphere 2 ran short of oxygen within two years), and a destination that can be made liveable. There are real first steps. Breakthrough Starshot is researching gram-scale laser-driven probes that might fly past Alpha Centauri, and astronomers have found candidate worlds nearby, such as Proxima b, though whether any could support life is unknown. A probe reaching another star would be the first real milestone.
