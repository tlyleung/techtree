A generation ship is a spacecraft built to carry a living community to another star on a voyage so long that the people who arrive are the descendants of those who left. With no faster propulsion in sight, it is the most physically plausible way for humans, rather than robotic probes, to reach other star systems, and it would make humanity a multi-star species.

The obstacles are immense. Voyager 1, the farthest human-made object, will come within 1.7 light-years of another star only in about 40,000 years, so a crewed ship would need far faster propulsion, likely fusion. It would also need artificial gravity, a closed ecosystem reliable for centuries, and a society stable enough to keep the mission going. Biosphere 2's two-year closed experiment steadily lost oxygen, and the best ground test since, China's Lunar Palace 1, ran for about a year. Researchers have estimated minimum crew sizes, and the 2025 Project Hyperion competition produced detailed habitat designs, but generation ships remain paper studies.
