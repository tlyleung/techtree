The Solar Age begins when a major economy gets most of its energy from sunlight: not just its electricity, but its energy overall, with heating, transport and industry running on it too. Earth receives vastly more energy from the Sun than civilisation uses, and solar is now the world's fastest-growing source of electricity. A country that ran mostly on solar would show the rest of the world what cheap, clean, domestic energy looks like, with cleaner air and far lower emissions. Growth has been remarkable: solar generation doubled in the three years to 2024, and in the first half of 2025 renewables overtook coal in the world's electricity for the first time. Yet electricity is only part of energy use. In 2025 solar supplied under 2% of the world's total energy and no more than about 7% in any country. Reaching half will need storage for night and winter, and electric cars, heat pumps and industrial processes on a vast scale.
